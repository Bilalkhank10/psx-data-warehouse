PSX Analytics — Pakistan Stock Exchange Data Warehouse

An end-to-end pipeline: a fault-tolerant Python extractor pulls 24,600+ daily price records for 20 PSX large-caps plus USD/PKR and SBP policy rates into DuckDB; a dbt layer models facts & dimensions — including a Type-2 slowly-changing dimension and ASOF currency joins; 41 automated tests and freshness SLAs guard quality; a five-tab Streamlit dashboard serves the insights, refreshed daily by GitHub Actions.
